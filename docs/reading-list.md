# Selected reading list

A curated starting point from the tutorial proposal. These readings provide historical foundations and a bridge to current language agents. This list will be expanded and annotated.

## Never-ending and continual learning

- Carlson, A., et al. (2010). **Toward an Architecture for Never-Ending Language Learning.** AAAI.
- Mitchell, T. M., et al. (2018). **Never-Ending Learning.** *Communications of the ACM*, 61(5), 103–115.
- Thrun, S. (1998). **Lifelong Learning Algorithms.** In *Learning to Learn*.
- De Lange, M., et al. (2022). **A Continual Learning Survey: Defying Forgetting in Classification Tasks.** *IEEE TPAMI*.

## Retrieval, memory, and changing knowledge

- Lewis, P., et al. (2020). **Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks.** NeurIPS.
- Asai, A., et al. (2024). **Self-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection.** ICLR. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11511)
- Chhikara, P., et al. (2025). **Mem0: Building Production-Ready AI Agents with Scalable Long-Term Memory.** [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19413)

## Tools, agent behavior, and feedback

- Yao, S., et al. (2023). **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models.** ICLR. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)
- Schick, T., et al. (2023). **Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools.** NeurIPS.
- Shinn, N., et al. (2024). **Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning.** NeurIPS.
- Madaan, A., et al. (2023). **Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback.** NeurIPS.

## Reliability and evaluation

- Yoran, O., et al. (2024). **AssistantBench: Can Web Agents Solve Realistic and Time-Consuming Tasks?** EMNLP.
- Tang, W., et al. (2025). **EvoWiki: Evaluating LLMs on Evolving Knowledge.** ACL.
- Wang, H., et al. (2026). **AgentSpec: Customizable Runtime Enforcement for Safe and Reliable LLM Agents.** ICSE. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18666)

For the published tutorial abstract and its citation metadata, see the [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.aacl-tutorials.4/).
